# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-28)


### ⚠ BREAKING CHANGES

* **config:** every part of theme.css is renamed to its path in the tree of ADR 110, as `launcher-mode-key` to `launcher > modes > button > kbd`, and the states :active, :urgent and :closing to :checked (or :default, ::selection, :state(today), :state(copied) where they meant those), :state(urgent) and :state(closing); :focus on a window and what is in its titlebar is window:focus-within. A theme in the old names is refused, with what to write instead.
* **config:** in the Mac way, the overview moves from Super+Up to Super+Ctrl+Up, the workspaces from Super+Left and Right to Super+Ctrl+Left and Right, and focusing and swapping tiles from Super+Ctrl(+Shift) with the arrows to Super+Ctrl+Alt(+Shift). Super with the arrows now reaches programs as Home and End. Bind the old chords in `keys.yaml` to keep them.
* **config:** the built-in keys move as above. A `keys.yaml` that unbinds Super+Escape, Super+Shift+R, Super+Shift+E, Alt+Tab, Alt+Shift+Tab or Super+Shift with a digit names a binding that is no longer there, and is reported; bindings of the user's own keep their keys, and any of the old ones can be bound again in `keys.yaml`.
* **config:** a theme's `button-close:hover`, `button-minimize:hover` and `button-maximize:hover` style the button with the pointer on it alone, where they styled all three with the pointer on any of them. A theme that wants the old look writes
* **ui:** with `output.scale: auto`, most laptops' own screens are drawn a step smaller than before and the densest monitors a step larger. The guide says how to have the old size back: give the number it was, as `output.scale` or for that screen in `outputs`.
* Super+F no longer makes a window fullscreen and Super+N no longer makes a workspace: they are Super+Ctrl+F and Super+Ctrl+N, and Super+Tab is the switcher rather than the next tab, now Super+`. A chord with Super that nothing binds reaches programs as Ctrl; set
